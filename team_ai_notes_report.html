Surendra Patel
AI Power User · 72/100
WHAT'S CLICKING

You get right down to business and use AI well to knock out boilerplate, clear up simple syntax issues, and keep momentum going when starting a task. You're also very receptive to picking up new workflow habits.

THE RELATABLE TRAP

Prompts lean heavily toward the happy path. When code looks clean on top, it's easy to assume the edge cases are covered, leaving verification to be handled manually later. It's also easy to fall back to manual print statements when pasting the actual error log into the model would resolve it much faster.
